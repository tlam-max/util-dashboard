Data Quality Review — Delivery Manager Checklist

Training & Certification · FY27 · Open the dashboard alongside this checklist

Open https://tlam-max.github.io/util-dashboard/ and the T&C Salesforce org side by side. Work through each section and tick items as you go. Flag anything that doesn't look right using the escalation table at the bottom.
Full team review

Review every instructor

Go through each name on the By Instructor tab one by one. Takes ~5 min per instructor. Recommended if this is the first review or you haven't checked in a while.

Spot check

Sample 2–3 instructors

Pick one high-volume instructor, one low-volume instructor, and one new hire. Verify their numbers in depth. Good for a quick confidence check each month.

1

Team total sanity check

  • By Manager tab → your team bar → does the total feel right for the months elapsed?
    Quick estimate: headcount × months active × 100 hrs. A ±30% gap is worth investigating.
  • Click your team bar → does the headcount in the modal match your actual active roster?
    Missing instructor = their Contact record likely lacks "Certified Instructor + Salesforce Employee + Active Contact" flags in Salesforce.
2

Per-instructor review

  • Click each instructor name (or your 2–3 spot-check picks) → does the monthly chart look reasonable? No unexplained spikes or zero months?
    A zero month for an active instructor usually means blank Start/End Time on a campaign in Salesforce, or campaigns not assigned to them.
  • AMER: click a monthly bar → do the campaign names and hours match real deliveries you know happened?
    Look for: the same campaign appearing twice (double-count), campaigns assigned to the wrong instructor, or campaigns with 0 hrs.
  • For a campaign that looks wrong — open it in Salesforce and verify these four fields:
    In Salesforce → Campaign record, check:
    1. Start_Time__c and End_Time__c — both should be in HH:MM:SS format. Blank = 0 hrs.
    2. StartDate and EndDate — should match the actual delivery days only, not span extra weekends.
    3. Address__c — should say a street address for onsite (gets 16 travel hrs) or contain "Virtual" for online (0 travel hrs).
    4. Instructor__c — confirms the right instructor is assigned to the campaign.
  • EMEA / APAC / LATAM: do monthly totals feel right? Cross-reference the FY27 Event Util Days Matrix Google Sheet for those months.
    No per-campaign drill-down available for these regions — the dashboard shows Google Sheet monthly aggregates only.
Red flags to look for
  • 0 hrs in a month the instructor was actively teaching → blank Start/End Time, or campaign not assigned to them
  • 300+ hrs in one month → multi-week course entered as one long campaign; should be split into one campaign per week
  • Travel hours (T) every month for a virtual-only instructor → Address field contains a street address, not "Virtual"
  • No travel hours for an instructor who regularly teaches onsite → Address field may incorrectly say "Virtual"
  • Same campaign appearing twice in a month's drill-down → duplicate campaign records in Salesforce
3

Hours calculation spot-check

  • Pick 2–3 recent campaigns for an AMER instructor. Do the hours match this formula?
    Final Util = (days × daily hrs × 1.3) + travel. Example: 5-day onsite, 8 hrs/day → (5 × 8 × 1.3) + 16 = 68 hrs.
  • Are multi-week courses entered as separate campaigns per week — not one long campaign spanning weeks?
    One campaign running Mon Week 1 → Fri Week 3 = 15 calendar days, inflating hours if there were non-teaching days in between.
4

Month to Plan (forward view)

  • In each instructor modal, does the Month to Plan section show upcoming campaigns already booked in future months?
    A future month showing 0 hrs means no campaigns are assigned yet in Salesforce — is that accurate, or are bookings missing?
  • Is the "% of prorated target" at the bottom consistent with where you'd expect the instructor to finish the year?
    Below 70% with few months left and nothing planned = a real pipeline gap, not a data issue. Above 100% = ahead of target.
5

New hire ramp dates

  • For each new hire, does the modal chart show the correct three phases — amber (0%), blue (50%), green (100%) — in the right months?
    Phases: months 1–3 = ramp (0 hrs target), months 4–6 = 50 hrs/month, month 7+ = 100 hrs/month. Misaligned shading = wrong start date in the data.
6

APAC / LATAM (Google Sheet source)

  • Do your APAC/LATAM instructors' monthly totals match the FY27 Event Util Days Matrix Google Sheet?
    Formula: util days × 8 hrs × 1.3 + travel. The dashboard number should match within rounding.
  • Are there any months where the Google Sheet has data but the dashboard shows zero — or vice versa?
    Either direction is a data load issue. Note the instructor name and month and escalate.
7

Attendees (By Impact tab)

  • Filter By Impact to your region → does the total attendee count look proportionate to your team's delivery volume?
    Rough check: sessions × average class size (~15–20 learners). A big gap = Attendee_Total__c field not being updated after delivery.
  • Click an instructor in the leaderboard → do per-campaign attendee counts look accurate? (A public course showing 1 attendee = data entry error.)
8

Course Primary Product (By Language tab)

Data quality action required in T&C Salesforce The By Language tab classifies every Authorization record to a cloud using Course__c.Primary_Product__c. The courses below have this field blank — they currently fall into Other in the dashboard. A Salesforce admin needs to set Primary_Product__c on these Course records.

How to fix: In T&C Salesforce, go to each Course record and set the Primary Product field to the correct cloud. Verify using the Course Code → Cloud Mapping Reference report (ID: 00OdK000003qKfFUAU · Global Authorization Reports folder). The report groups all certs by Primary Product — courses under (blank) are the ones to fix.
