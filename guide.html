Overview

This dashboard gives a worldwide view of the T&C Delivery Team's utilization health for FY27. It combines Salesforce campaign data with event data from the Google Sheet to produce a single, consistent utilization number per instructor.

The headline number: each instructor targets 1,200 utilization hours per year (100 hrs/month). Hours include teaching time with a 30% prep multiplier, plus 16 hrs for each onsite delivery. New hires are pro-rated after a 3-month ramp.

Quick Tips for Managers

The most important nuances to know before reading the full guide.

1 · A month bar can look high even with few campaigns listed
Clicking a bar shows only Salesforce campaigns. Hours from Boot Camps, travel, and private workshops tracked in the Google Sheet do not appear as individual campaigns — they are loaded as monthly aggregates. If the hours shown in the bar are higher than the campaign list explains, cross-reference the Google Sheet (FY27 Event Util Days Matrix) to see the full activity breakdown for that month.
2 · New hire % of target is intentionally lower — that's by design
New hires follow a 3-phase ramp schedule:
  • Months 1–3 (0–3 months in role): 0% target — onboarding period, no billable hours expected
  • Months 4–6 (3–6 months in role): 50% target — 50 hrs/month, building their delivery load
  • Month 7+ (6+ months in role): 100% target — full 100 hrs/month
Their FY27 target is pro-rated accordingly — a May 2026 starter has a full-year target of 450 hrs (3 × 50 + 3 × 100), not 1,200. A low % on a new hire's tile is normal and expected during their ramp period.
3 · Pace vs Target (YTD) only counts hours up to the current month
The Pace tile on an instructor's modal compares their YTD actual (through the current month only) against their YTD expected based on their ramp schedule. December and January data are not included in the pace calculation until those months arrive. The sub-label always shows exactly which month it is calculated through (e.g. "900 hrs expected through Nov 26").
4 · Campaign drill-down is AMER only
The per-campaign breakdown when clicking a monthly bar is only available for AMER instructors (sourced from Salesforce). For EMEA, APAC, and LATAM instructors, all data comes from the Google Sheet as monthly aggregates — clicking a bar will show "no Salesforce campaign data" for these regions.
5 · Use "New Hires: Remove" to see your established team's true pace
Toggling New Hires: Remove in the header hides all FY27 new hires from every view — KPIs, charts, the target line, and all tabs. The worldwide target line drops to reflect only the established team's targets. This gives you a cleaner read on how the core team is tracking without new hire ramp noise pulling the numbers down.
6 · Drill from manager → instructor → back without losing your place
Click a manager bar to open their team modal. Click any instructor name in the team table to see their individual metrics. A ← Back to team button appears at the top of the instructor modal — click it to return directly to the manager's team view without closing and reopening.
7 · Utilization hours include a 30% prep multiplier
Hours are not raw teaching time. Each delivery day is multiplied by 1.3 to credit instructors for preparation time. On-site deliveries also add 16 travel hours. This means a 5-day course (40 hrs teaching) contributes 52 hrs utilization, plus travel if on-site. The Google Sheet column Final_Util__c is the field the dashboard sums.

Calculated Fields (Salesforce)

Four formula fields were created on the Campaign object in the T&C Salesforce org. All four are visible in the CRT report and in individual Campaign records (read-only for Salesforce Employee and Contractor profiles).

Duration_Hours__c — Daily Teaching Hours
IF( AND(LEN(Start_Time__c) >= 5, LEN(End_Time__c) >= 5), ROUND( (VALUE(LEFT(End_Time__c,2)) + VALUE(MID(End_Time__c,4,2))/60) - (VALUE(LEFT(Start_Time__c,2)) + VALUE(MID(Start_Time__c,4,2))/60), 1 ), NULL )
What it does: Converts the Start_Time__c and End_Time__c text fields (stored as HH:MM:SS) into decimal hours per teaching day. Example: 09:00 to 17:00 = 8.0 hrs. Returns NULL if either field is missing.
Actual_Util__c — Total Teaching Hours
IF( AND(NOT(ISBLANK(StartDate)), NOT(ISBLANK(EndDate)), LEN(Start_Time__c) >= 5, LEN(End_Time__c) >= 5), ROUND( (EndDate - StartDate + 1) * ((VALUE(LEFT(End_Time__c,2)) + VALUE(MID(End_Time__c,4,2))/60) - (VALUE(LEFT(Start_Time__c,2)) + VALUE(MID(Start_Time__c,4,2))/60)), 1 ), NULL )
What it does: Multiplies the number of calendar days in the campaign (EndDate - StartDate + 1) by the daily teaching hours. A 3-day campaign running 09:00–17:00 = 3 × 8 = 24 hrs. Weekends are included because bootcamps can run on weekends and each delivery block is its own separate campaign record. Returns NULL if dates or times are missing.
Travel_Hours__c — Onsite Travel Allowance
IF( AND(NOT(ISBLANK(Address__c)), NOT(CONTAINS(Address__c, "Virtual"))), 16, 0 )
What it does: Awards 16 hours of travel credit for any onsite campaign. The detection uses the campaign's Address field, not the campaign name — virtual deliveries have "Virtual" in their address; onsite deliveries have a real street/city address. 16 hrs = 2 travel days × 8 hrs.
Final_Util__c — Final Utilization Hours
ROUND(Actual_Util__c * 1.3 + Travel_Hours__c, 1)
What it does: The master utilization number per campaign. Teaching hours are multiplied by 1.3 (30% prep multiplier) to credit instructors for preparation time alongside delivery time. Travel hours are then added. This is the field summed in the dashboard and in the Salesforce report chart's reference line.

Target derivation: 12.7 teach days/month × 8 hrs × 1.3 prep = 132.1 hrs/month teaching+prep, plus travel varies. For target-setting, the number was simplified to 100 hrs/month / 1,200 hrs/year — this is the reference line value used in the Salesforce report chart and in this dashboard.

New Hire Logic

Instructors who joined during FY27 have a three-phase ramp applied to their monthly targets. Rather than holding a new hire to the same 100 hrs/month as the established team, their target steps up gradually across their first six months.

Three-phase ramp schedule

PhaseMonthsMonthly targetFY27 contribution
Ramp Start month through start + 2 (first 3 months) 0 hrs — no billable expectation Learning delivery format, shadowing, co-facilitating
50% billing Start + 3 through start + 5 (next 3 months) 50 hrs/month Building toward full delivery load
Full billing Start + 6 onwards 100 hrs/month Full utilization target applies

Example — Aug 2026 start: Ramp Aug–Oct (0 hrs). 50% billing Nov–Jan = 3 × 50 = 150 hrs FY27 target. Full billing starts Feb 2027 (FY28). Without ramp, a simple pro-rate from Aug would give 600 hrs — this tiered approach reflects the reality of how long it takes a new instructor to reach full capacity.

Example — Apr 2026 start: Ramp Apr–Jun (0 hrs). 50% billing Jul–Sep = 3 × 50 = 150 hrs. Full billing Oct–Jan = 4 × 100 = 400 hrs. FY27 target = 550 hrs.

Edge cases

  • Melody Yang & Javier Garcia Salcedo — Feb 2026 start (FY27 start). Their ramp runs Feb–Apr, 50% phase May–Jul, full billing from Aug 2026.
  • Aug 2026 starters — Full billing phase (month 7+) falls in Feb 2027, which is FY28. Their FY27 target is capped at the 50% phase: 150 hrs.
  • Candice Belandres (Jul 2026 start) — Full billing starts Jan 2027, giving one full-rate month in FY27.

How this appears in the instructor modal chart

The monthly breakdown chart uses background shading and colored boundary lines to show all three phases at a glance:

  • Amber shading over the ramp months (phase 1). Dashed amber vertical line at the start month. Tick labels turn amber.
  • Blue shading over the 50% months (phase 2). Dashed blue vertical line at the 50% start. Tick labels turn blue.
  • Solid green vertical line at the 100% billing start (phase 3). Tick label turns green. If the 100% start falls outside FY27, a note says "100% in FY28".
  • The target line on the chart steps: flat at 0 during ramp, steps up to 50 during the 50% phase, then to 100 at full billing.
  • A legend row below the chart shows the exact date ranges for each phase.
  • The FY27 Target KPI tile subtitle breaks out the contribution, e.g. "3mo × 50hrs, 1mo × 100hrs".
  • The modal subtitle reads: New hire [month] · 50% from [month] · 100% from [month] (or "100% in FY28").

New Hire Start Dates source

Start dates come from the "New Hire Start Dates" tab in the Google Sheet Excel file. The 16 instructors listed there are flagged as new hires in the dashboard.

New Hires toggle

The New Hires: Remove button in the top-right header hides or shows all new hires across every view — KPI tiles, trend chart, target line, By Region, By Manager, and By Instructor tabs. When active (label changes to New Hires: Removed), the worldwide target line drops to reflect only the established headcount and their full-rate targets.

The toggle also affects modals: when Removed, new hires are excluded from the manager team table and KPIs, and an amber banner appears on a new hire's individual modal to indicate they are currently excluded from team views.

How cloud classification works

Because there is no dedicated cloud/product field on Campaign records in Salesforce, the dashboard classifies each campaign by extracting a course code from the campaign name and matching it to a known prefix. The process is:

  1. Extract the course code from the campaign name (e.g. AMER - TTD101 - 2026-05-07 → TTD101). The extractor handles codes between dashes, spaces, or parentheses, and at the end of the name.
  2. Match the alphabetic prefix of the code (TTD) to the cloud mapping table.
  3. If no code is found, fall back to keyword scanning of the full campaign name (e.g. "Anypoint" → MuleSoft, "Health Cloud" → Industries).
  4. If neither matches, the campaign goes into Other.

The "Other" detail panel — monitoring unclassified campaigns

Click the Other bar in the By Cloud view to see the full list of unclassified campaigns, sorted by hours. The panel header shows the count of unclassified campaigns. Use this list to:

  • Identify campaigns with unusual naming that could be re-classified with a prefix rule change
  • Spot bootcamp and HOT records to monitor their volume over time
  • Share with the Ops team as evidence for data cleanup requests

Each row shows the full campaign name, hours, and percentage of the Other total. As campaigns get correctly classified (either through naming fixes or by adding a Cloud__c field in Salesforce), they will automatically disappear from this list on the next data refresh.

Known Issues & Follow-ups

Action required: The items below are open gaps that need follow-up. A full list of all data quality issues found during this project — plus the clean-data standards required for FY28 — is documented in the T&C Data Quality & FY28 Requirements document.

APAC / LATAM campaigns missing from the Salesforce report

What you see

Instructors on Affa Khatoon's, Mohina Sheikh's, and Amanda Ortega's teams do not appear in the "FY27 NEW Delivery Util report*" — even though filters 15 and 16 in the report contain all their full names.

Root cause

APAC and LATAM campaigns are created and owned by the TandC Integrations integration user (a system account, not a person). The instructor who delivers the campaign is stored in a custom field: Campaign.Instructor__c — a Contact lookup field, not Campaign Owner.

The report type "Campaigns with Contacts with User" (Campaigns_with_Contacts_with_User__c) joins Campaign → User via Campaign Owner only. It does not traverse Campaign.Instructor__c. As a result:

  • User.Name filters match the Campaign Owner, which is "TandC Integrations" for all APAC/LATAM campaigns.
  • No instructor name filter will ever match these records in the current report type, regardless of what names are listed in the filter.

Confirmed example: Campaign "APAC - ADX201 - 16 Feb" — Owner: TandC Integrations, Instructor__c: Shahbaz Nasimi (Contact). Shahbaz is in filter 15, but this campaign cannot appear in the report.

Impact

  • The Salesforce report does not show any APAC or LATAM utilization data for this team.
  • The dashboard's "By Cloud" sub-views (By Manager, By Instructor) show "No AMER campaign data" for Affa, Mohina, and Amanda's teams — because the camps data used for By Cloud is also sourced from Campaign Owner.
  • AMER and EMEA are not affected — their campaigns use Campaign Owner as the instructor identifier.

Options to fix

OptionEffortNotes
A · New custom report type Medium Create a new CRT that joins Campaign → Contact via Campaign.Instructor__c, then Contact → User via Contact.User__c. This is the cleanest fix and would make filters work properly. Requires Salesforce admin access in Setup → Report Types.
B · Change Campaign Owner for APAC/LATAM Medium–High Update the TandC Integrations integration to set Campaign Owner = the instructor's User record instead of (or in addition to) Instructor__c. Would make the existing report type work without changes. Risk: may affect other automation that depends on TandC Integrations being the owner.
C · Export via separate report / SOQL Low Create a separate Salesforce report or SOQL query that reads Campaign.Instructor__c directly, then merge the APAC/LATAM data into the dashboard alongside the AMER/EMEA data. Quickest workaround without touching the report type or integration.

Current workaround: APAC and LATAM utilization data is sourced from the Google Sheet (FY27 Event Util Days Matrix) as monthly aggregates. This is why APAC/LATAM instructors appear in the main dashboard tabs (By Region, By Manager, By Instructor) but not in the By Cloud views — the Google Sheet does not include a cloud/product breakdown per delivery.

FAQ

Why doesn't the campaign drill-down work for EMEA/APAC instructors?

EMEA, APAC, and LATAM instructors who deliver events have their hours recorded in the Google Sheet, not as individual Salesforce campaign records. The Google Sheet only captures monthly totals, not per-event detail. AMER instructors are 100% in Salesforce and have full per-campaign drill-down.

Why are Internal campaigns in Bootcamp?

Internal campaigns represent beta teaches — where instructors deliver a course internally before it goes to customers. This counts toward utilization and is grouped with Bootcamp since both represent non-standard delivery formats beyond the core Public/Workshop/Private Workshop types.

Why is the travel detection based on the Address field, not the campaign name?

Most virtual campaigns do not include the word "virtual" in their name. The reliable signal is the campaign's Address field: virtual deliveries have "Virtual" in the address (e.g. "Virtual i-Learning"), while onsite deliveries have a real street address or city. The formula checks NOT(CONTAINS(Address__c, "Virtual")).

Why is the target 1,200 and not 1,524 (12.7 days × 8 hrs × 1.3 × 12 months)?

The 1,524 figure includes the prep multiplier in the teaching component. The 1,200 target was established as the practical planning number — a rounded-down simplification that provides a clean reference line value. It was set at 1,200 (100/month) for ease of communication and is the value used in both the Salesforce report chart and this dashboard.

How is a new hire's FY27 target calculated with the tiered ramp?

The target is the sum of each month's individual contribution: 0 hrs for ramp months, 50 hrs for 50% phase months, and 100 hrs for full billing months — counting only months that fall within FY27 (Feb 2026 – Jan 2027). For example, an April 2026 starter has: 0+0+0 (ramp, Apr–Jun) + 50+50+50 (50% phase, Jul–Sep) + 100+100+100+100 (full billing, Oct–Jan) = 550 hrs FY27 target.

Why is Japan excluded?

The Japan team operates separately and is not part of the worldwide T&C Delivery Team being measured here. Their campaigns exist in Salesforce but are excluded from this dashboard by region filter.

How do I refresh the data?

The dashboard is a self-contained HTML file with the data baked in. To refresh: re-run the Python build script (which re-pulls the Salesforce report and re-reads the Google Sheet Excel file), then copy the new HTML file to replace the existing one. No server or database is required.

Can I share this file?

Yes — it is a single self-contained HTML file with no external data dependencies (only Chart.js, loaded from a CDN). You can email it, put it on a shared drive, or host it on an internal page. Anyone who opens it gets the same view. Because the data is embedded, there is no live connection to Salesforce — the file reflects the point-in-time data from when it was last built.
