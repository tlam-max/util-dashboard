1 Report Overview

The report is built on a custom report type called Campaigns with Contacts with User. It traverses three objects:

User → Contact (via Instructor_User_Record__c) → Campaign (via Instructor__c)

Each row represents one campaign delivery assigned to an instructor. The report is filtered to show only active, certified Salesforce FTE instructors (see Section 2) and aggregated by instructor to produce individual and team-level utilization totals.

3 Manager Group Filters (Hardcoded Names)

The report uses a second layer of filters — filters 10 through 16 — to scope results to only the instructors on the worldwide delivery team. These are User.Name contains filters, one per manager group, combined with OR logic so a campaign is included if the Campaign Owner matches any of the seven groups.

Why hardcode names instead of using a Manager field? The report type only exposes 9 User fields and does not traverse the User → Manager relationship (i.e. User.Manager.Name is not available). The Contact object's Manager__c field is only populated for Candidate Instructors, not Certified Instructors. Hardcoding full names in User.Name contains filters was the lowest-risk approach that works reliably within the current report type.

The full boolean logic across all 16 filters is:

1 AND 2 AND 3 AND 4 AND 5 AND 6 AND 7 AND 8 AND 9 AND (10 OR 11 OR 12 OR 13 OR 14 OR 15 OR 16 OR 17 OR 18)

Filters 1–9 are the base criteria from Section 2. Filters 10–18 are the manager groups below — one filter per manager.

4 Calculated Fields

Four formula fields were created on the Campaign object to drive the utilization calculation. All are available as columns in the report.

Duration Hours

Daily teaching hours for a campaign

Computed from the campaign's Start Time and End Time fields (stored as text in HH:MM:SS format). Extracts hours and minutes separately, converts to decimal hours, and rounds to one decimal place.

IF(AND(LEN(Start_Time__c) >= 5, LEN(End_Time__c) >= 5), ROUND( (VALUE(LEFT(End_Time__c,2)) + VALUE(MID(End_Time__c,4,2))/60) - (VALUE(LEFT(Start_Time__c,2)) + VALUE(MID(Start_Time__c,4,2))/60), 1), NULL)
09:00 → 17:00→8.0 hrs
09:30 → 18:00→8.5 hrs
09:00 → 13:00→4.0 hrs
Actual Util Hours

Total teaching hours for the campaign period

Multiplies the number of calendar days in the campaign by the daily teaching hours. Calendar days are used (not business days) because some bootcamps run on weekends. Each campaign represents one contiguous delivery block — multi-week courses are entered as separate campaigns per week, so there are no embedded non-teaching weekends to exclude.

ROUND( (EndDate - StartDate + 1) × Duration_Hours__c, 1)
Mon–Fri, 8.5 hrs/day→5 × 8.5 = 42.5 hrs
Sat–Sun bootcamp, 8 hrs/day→2 × 8 = 16.0 hrs
Travel Hours

16 hours added for any onsite delivery

Instructors traveling to deliver in person receive an allowance of 2 travel days (16 hours) per campaign. Whether a delivery is onsite is determined by the campaign's Address field — not the campaign name. Virtual deliveries always contain the word "Virtual" in the address; onsite deliveries have a physical street address.

IF(AND( NOT(ISBLANK(Address__c)), NOT(CONTAINS(Address__c, "Virtual")) ), 16, 0)
Why Address__c and not the campaign name? Some private workshops have "Virtual" in their name but are delivered at a client's physical location (e.g., "CCD102 - PW - Virtual - Luigi Lavazza"). The Address field reliably distinguishes online from onsite regardless of naming conventions.
Final Util Hours

Complete utilization figure per campaign

Combines teaching hours (with prep multiplier) and travel hours into a single number.

ROUND(Actual_Util__c × 1.3 + Travel_Hours__c, 1)

The 1.3 multiplier adds 30% to actual teaching hours to account for course preparation time — reviewing materials, updating content, setting up labs — which is real work time not captured in the delivery calendar.

6 Yearly Utilization Target

The team's monthly target is 12.7 teaching days per month. To convert this to an annual hours target:

1,200
hours / year (annual utilization target)
12.7 days/month × 8 hrs/day = 101.6 hrs/month 101.6 hrs/month × 12 months = 1,219.2 hrs/year → rounded down to 1,200 hrs/year

The 1,200 hour line is shown as a reference line in the report chart, providing a visual benchmark to quickly identify instructors who are on track, above, or below target for the fiscal year.

Note on rounding The target is rounded down (not to the nearest integer) to give instructors a slight buffer and keep the benchmark a clean, communicable number.
Teaching Only toggle — seeing actual teach time vs. the target The dashboard's Util: Teaching Only button (top-right header) strips both the 1.3× prep multiplier and travel days from all displayed hours. The 100 hrs/month target stays fixed in both modes. This lets you compare raw customer-facing teaching hours directly against the same target, making it immediately visible how much of each instructor's utilization progress comes from the prep credit and travel allowance versus actual time delivering to customers. Toggle back to Util: With Multiplier to restore the official utilization view.

8 Known Issue: APAC / LATAM Missing Data

What you see

Instructors on the APAC and LATAM teams (filters 15 and 16) do not appear in the report results — even though the filters are correctly configured with their full names.

Root cause

APAC and LATAM campaigns are created and owned by a system integration account called TandC Integrations. The instructor who delivers the session is stored in a custom field on the Campaign object: Campaign.Instructor__c — a Contact lookup, not Campaign Owner.

The report type joins Campaign → User via Campaign Owner only. It does not traverse Campaign.Instructor__c. Because of this:

  • User.Name filters match against the Campaign Owner — which is always "TandC Integrations" for APAC/LATAM.
  • No instructor name filter will ever match these records in the current report type, regardless of what names are listed.
  • Filters 15 and 16 are structurally correct but produce zero results because the data relationship they depend on does not exist in the current report type.
Confirmed example Campaign "APAC - ADX201 - 16 Feb" — Owner: TandC Integrations, Instructor__c: Shahbaz Nasimi (Contact). Shahbaz is listed in filter 15, but the campaign does not appear in report results.

Options to fix

OptionEffortDetails
A · New custom report type Medium Create a new CRT that joins Campaign → Contact via Campaign.Instructor__c, then Contact → User via Contact.User__c. Cleanest fix — filters would work as intended. Requires Salesforce admin access in Setup → Report Types.
B · Update Campaign Owner in the integration Medium–High Change the TandC Integrations automation to set Campaign Owner = the instructor's User record instead of (or alongside) Instructor__c. Makes the existing report type work without changes. Risk: may affect other automation that depends on TandC Integrations being the owner.
C · Separate SOQL export for APAC/LATAM Low Write a separate Salesforce report or SOQL query that reads Campaign.Instructor__c directly, then merge the data downstream. Quickest workaround without touching the report type or integration.

Current workaround

APAC and LATAM utilization data is sourced separately from the Google Sheet (FY27 Event Util Days Matrix) as monthly aggregates. This is why these instructors appear in the dashboard's main views (By Region, By Manager, By Instructor) but are missing from campaign-level drill-downs and the By Cloud views — the Google Sheet does not include a per-cloud or per-campaign breakdown.
