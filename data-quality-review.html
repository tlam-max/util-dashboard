Data Quality Review — Delivery Manager Checklist

Training & Certification · FY27 · Open the dashboard alongside this checklist

How to use this: Open https://tlam-max.github.io/util-dashboard/, filter to your team on the By Instructor tab, and work through each section. Tick each item as you go. Flag anything that doesn't look right using the escalation table at the bottom.
1

Team total sanity check

  • By Manager tab → your team bar → does the total feel right for the months elapsed?
    Quick estimate: headcount × months active × 100 hrs. A ±30% gap is worth investigating.
  • Click your team bar → confirm the headcount in the modal matches your actual active roster.
    Missing instructor = their Contact record likely lacks "Certified Instructor + Salesforce Employee + Active" flags.
2

Per-instructor review

  • Click each instructor name → does their monthly chart look reasonable with no unexplained spikes or zero months?
    A zero month for an active instructor usually means blank Start/End Time on a campaign, or campaigns weren't assigned to them.
  • AMER: click each monthly bar → do the campaign names and hours match real deliveries?
    Look for duplicates (same campaign twice) or campaigns belonging to a different instructor.
  • EMEA: do monthly totals feel right? (No per-campaign drill-down — cross-reference Google Sheet if in doubt.)
Red flags
  • 0 hrs for an active teaching month → missing campaign data or blank time fields
  • 300+ hrs in one month → multi-week course entered as one long campaign instead of weekly splits
  • Travel hours every month for a virtual-only instructor → Address field has a street address instead of "Virtual"
  • No travel hours for an instructor who regularly teaches onsite → Address field may say "Virtual" incorrectly
3

Hours calculation spot-check

  • Pick 2–3 recent campaigns for an AMER instructor. Do the hours match this formula?
    Final Util = (days × daily hrs × 1.3) + travel. Example: 5-day onsite, 8 hrs/day → (5 × 8 × 1.3) + 16 = 68 hrs.
  • Are multi-week courses entered as separate campaigns per week — not one long campaign spanning weeks?
    One campaign running Mon Week 1 → Fri Week 3 = 15 calendar days, inflating hours if there were non-teaching days in between.
4

Month to Plan (forward view)

  • In each instructor modal, does the Month to Plan section show upcoming campaigns already booked in future months?
    A future month showing 0 hrs means no campaigns are assigned yet in Salesforce — is that accurate, or are bookings missing?
  • Is the "% of prorated target" at the bottom consistent with where you'd expect the instructor to finish the year?
    Below 70% with few months left and nothing planned = a real pipeline gap, not a data issue. Above 100% = ahead of target.
5

New hire ramp dates

  • For each new hire, does the modal chart show the correct three phases — amber (0%), blue (50%), green (100%) — in the right months?
    Phases: months 1–3 = ramp (0 hrs target), months 4–6 = 50 hrs/month, month 7+ = 100 hrs/month. Misaligned shading = wrong start date in the data.
6

APAC / LATAM (Google Sheet source)

  • Do your APAC/LATAM instructors' monthly totals match the FY27 Event Util Days Matrix Google Sheet?
    Formula: util days × 8 hrs × 1.3 + travel. The dashboard number should match within rounding.
  • Are there any months where the Google Sheet has data but the dashboard shows zero — or vice versa?
    Either direction is a data load issue. Note the instructor name and month and escalate.
7

Attendees (By Impact tab)

  • Filter By Impact to your region → does the total attendee count look proportionate to your team's delivery volume?
    Rough check: sessions × average class size (~15–20 learners). A big gap = Attendee_Total__c field not being updated after delivery.
  • Click an instructor in the leaderboard → do per-campaign attendee counts look accurate? (A public course showing 1 attendee = data entry error.)
